From the owner

My chacha gave me this chair in 1999

I was a kid sweeping hair off the floor when my chacha opened this shop near the Rohtak bus stand in 1999. I still remember the first customer, a bus conductor who paid and left a smile. That was enough to teach me what this work is about.

Today I have over ten years of my own experience behind the chair. Fashions change, fades come and go, but the thing people want is the same: a clean cut, honest rate, and no waiting around.

Come as you are, with dust from the journey or in your wedding clothes. We will take care of you the same way.

  • Haircut at just ₹60, the rate is on the board and never changes at the chair
  • Over 10 years of hands-on experience with every kind of cut
  • Open since 1999, right at Rohtak main bus stand
